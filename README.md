# 📸 Live Photo

**Turn your videos into Live Photos.**

Live Photo is an Android application designed to transform ordinary videos into Live Photo-style moments, with customizable frames, looping playback, and high-quality exports.

## ✨ Features

- 🎬 **Video to Live Photo** – Convert videos into Live Photo-style creations.
- ✂️ **Video Trimming** – Select the exact moment you want.
- 🖼️ **Cover Frame Selection** – Choose your preferred thumbnail.
- 🔁 **Loop Playback** – Enjoy seamless looping.
- 💎 **High-Quality Export** – Preserve visual quality.
- 📂 **Gallery Saving** – Save creations directly to your device.
- 📤 **Sharing** – Share your creations with others.

## 🛠️ Built With

- Kotlin
- Jetpack Compose
- Android SDK
- Gradle

## 📱 Platform

- Android
- Package: `com.livephoto.app`

iOS support is a future platform goal.
